# CDEadmin hard-fork and attribution notice

CDEadmin is an independent hard fork of pgAdmin 4, based on pgAdmin 4 9.17.
It retains portions of the pgAdmin 4 source code, packaging, tests,
documentation structure, and user interface.

pgAdmin 4 is copyright (C) 2013-2026 The pgAdmin Development Team and is
distributed under the PostgreSQL Licence. The upstream `LICENSE` file and
platform-specific license notices must remain in every derived distribution.

The active product name and visual identity are CDEadmin. References to
`pgadmin`, `pgAdmin`, `PGADMIN_`, and `pgAdmin4.py` remain only where required
as compatibility identifiers inherited from upstream or where pgAdmin 4 is
being credited. They are not CDEadmin product branding.

CDEadmin-specific copyright, trademark, artwork, publisher, update endpoint,
and signing-key statements remain subject to product-owner, legal, security,
and release-engineering approval. The hard-fork status is settled; a release
is not yet approved until the remaining release controls are completed.
